VOIDCARVE is installed!

USING IT IN FL STUDIO
1. Options > Manage plugins > click "Find more plugins" (or "Start scan").
   VoidCarve appears under Effects.
2. Put VoidCarve on the mixer insert of the sound you want to make room in
   (e.g. your beat, pads or guitars).
3. Select the mixer insert of the sound that should cut through
   (e.g. vocals or kick). Right-click the small arrow at the bottom of the
   VoidCarve track and choose "Sidechain to this track".
4. Open VoidCarve, click the gear/cog icon (plugin wrapper settings), go to
   the Processing tab and set the sidechain input to the track you just routed.
5. The status light turns green ("SIDECHAIN LIVE") when it hears the sidechain.
   Turn up AMOUNT until the vocal/kick pops out.

TIPS
- Vocals: Low Cut ~150 Hz, High Cut ~8 kHz, Attack 5-15 ms, Release 100-200 ms.
- Kick vs bass: High Cut ~200 Hz, fast attack, release 60-120 ms.
- If nothing happens, raise SENSE.
